Meet the speakers

Isma Khemies

Leadership trainer, coach and consultant

Supports women leaders and founders in tech, after 15+ years leading multicultural teams across Europe and North America.

Read bioHide bio

Isma became a leader at 27, managing 21 people from 22 countries, in a foreign country and a non-native language, with strong imposter syndrome. She knows how the urge to prove yourself turns into overwork, and how overwork ends in burnout.

In her opening keynote, Isma shows why rest is part of productivity and how to lead without running yourself empty.

Burnout

International Healing Coach, founder of the Healing Academy

Best-selling author, Stevie Award winner in Women in Business and CEO of Self · Health · Wealth.

Read bioHide bio

After 11 years living with autoimmune disease, rheumatoid arthritis, fibromyalgia, epilepsy, ADHD and chronic pain, Maria healed completely and rebuilt her life.

Drawing on 17+ years of research, she combines Movement Medicine, nutrition science, somatic healing and energy work to address the root cause of symptoms. She has guided more than 200 students in over 24 countries.

Leadership coach, founder of Aspira Consultancy

Own Your Inner Oracle – Intuitive Leadership

with Irina Irimia

Learn to trust the quiet intelligence you already carry. Irina guides you to read your inner signals and use intuition as a practical leadership tool, for clearer decisions and a steadier presence in the moments that matter.

Read bioHide bio

Irina describes herself as a lifelong student of consciousness and alchemy. For the past 17 years, she has studied and integrated psychosomatics, applied neuroscience and various transpersonal healing practices into her therapeutic work, helping individuals transform pain into inner sovereignty and prosperity.

Her primary passion lies in the synergy of travel, learning and teaching. In 2024, she delivered a workshop at Oxford University during the largest transpersonal psychology conference worldwide. She also volunteers to empower women by reminding them of their inner wisdom and strength.

She is in her fourth year of a master’s programme at the Institute of Transpersonal Psychology in Barcelona and a recent graduate of Dr. Alberto Villoldo’s Shamanic Medicine Energy School. She is also conducting international research on women’s intuitive intelligence.

Lead with Confidence: At Work and at Home

How to inspire, empower and guide your teenager into adulthood

with Ria Berkhout

You already have the qualities of a great leader. Now discover how to use them to strengthen your family and empower your teenager.

Read bioHide bio

Ria Berkhout is an author, certified Louise Hay trainer and family confidence coach with over 30 years of experience in personal development, teaching and coaching.

She empowers parents to become confident leaders within their families, strengthening trust, communication and relationships while guiding teenagers towards a confident, resilient and independent adulthood.

What Being a Casino Director Taught Me About High-Impact Leadership

with Carmen Cornelia Haselwanter

As AI transforms the way we live, work and lead, Carmen shares what eight years running the casino in St. Moritz taught her: our greatest strength isn't competing with technology, but embracing what makes us human. Empathy, authenticity, trust and the ability to create meaningful connection.

“Technology may change the way we work, but Human Connection changes the way we lead.”

Read bioHide bio

Voice of Human Connection · International Speaker · Leadership & Personal Growth Mentor · Former Casino Director, St. Moritz

With 30+ years of international leadership experience across hospitality, tourism and entertainment, including eight years as Casino Director in St. Moritz, Switzerland, Carmen knows first-hand that exceptional leadership, meaningful relationships, high-performing teams and outstanding customer experiences have one thing in common: genuine human connection. Having lived and worked across countries and cultures, she has found one universal truth: people thrive when they feel seen, heard and valued.

Today Carmen helps individuals, entrepreneurs, leaders and organisations rediscover the power of Human Connection, starting with themselves. Through her Human Connection Framework and her ALL-IN Mastery® methodology, she helps people turn insight into purposeful decisions, practical strategies and lasting results, through mentoring, leadership development, interactive workshops and corporate training.

Discover more about Carmen and her work at carmen-cornelia-haselwanter.com.

Meet your hosts

Organiser

Katarzyna Krawiecka

Katarzyna Krawiecka is a UK-based memoir ghostwriter and the founder of Legacy Architect. She writes books for founders, CEOs and leaders who have built something significant and want the story behind it told in their own voice.

Through recorded conversations using her IMPRINT method, she draws out the moments that shaped a life and turns them into a book for their family, their team or their industry. She is the author of six published books, the host of the Legacy Talks podcast and a member of the Union of Polish Writers Abroad. Katarzyna believes every life holds a story worth keeping, and that it should be written while it can still be told.

Moderator

Andra Curelariu

Andra Curelariu is an international entrepreneur who shares her time between her two passions: communication and design, and body alignment and rejuvenation.

Since 2008 she has run a non-governmental, non-profit organisation that fosters non-formal education for young people across Europe, values networking and shares the vision of growing beautifully together in this world full of wonders.
